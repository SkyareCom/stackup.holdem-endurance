## English

**Name**  
STACKUP ENDURANCE

**Short description — 71 characters**  
Mental performance for poker: focus, discipline, sessions and recovery.

**Full description**  
STACKUP ENDURANCE is a guided mental-performance training system for poker players.

The app organizes preparation, play, monitoring, breaks, recovery and review into one clear journey. Instead of presenting disconnected tools, it helps you understand your current state, interpret performance signals and choose the next practical action.

Current-release features:
- pre-session Ready Check;
- process goals;
- A/B/C Game monitoring;
- Decision Cues and check-ins;
- BREAK 4 recovery protocol;
- post-session debrief and Battle Diary;
- War Room and emotional heatmap;
- Mental Gym, Behavior Lab and focus training;
- Mental Audio with usage guidance;
- local contextual Coach;
- Portuguese, English and Spanish interface.

The goal is to protect decision quality through demanding sessions by improving consistency, attention, discipline and recovery.

STACKUP ENDURANCE does not provide betting, real-money gameplay, poker-room access or any mechanism to place wagers. It is a training and performance-organization tool.
